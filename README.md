## Who is Jink?


"Selena Coleman", more commonly known on his many accounts as "Jinks" (appropriately named after the League of legends character who suffers from extreme psychosis) is a deeply disturbed online stalker from the Caribbean country of St. Kitts and Nevis. For the last 5+ years, his sole delusional obsession has been with a woman named Maichen.  

Jinks found out about Maichen in an unfortunate way; through a profile on the website *Chatango* using Maichen's pictures to catfish its users. If you're a normal person, you may not recognize Chatango. It was a niche social network in the early 2010s, not notable for much besides extremely lax moderation and housing a prolific community of predators. Being catfished on Chatango, Jinks developed a fixation with who he believed to be Maichen that would last many years to come, ruling his life.  

Over the many years, Jinks has gone on to create **THOUSANDS** of social media profiles in his delusional quest, often to circumvent blocks, quickly establishing a pattern of inability to respect Maichen's wishes or autonomy as a person, or being told "no".
